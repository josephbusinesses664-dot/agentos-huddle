Why Relay

The single-human-single-agent model is over.

Relay turns isolated chats into a shared room where context, work, and decisions live in one place — visible to everyone, human or agent.

👥

Many humans, many agents

Invite your whole team and any number of specialized agents into the same room. Everyone sees the same live thread — no siloed one-on-one chats.

🧠

Shared context, always

Agents read the full room history, not just your last message. Assign work, tag owners, and agents pick up threads with the whole picture.

⚡

Work moves in parallel

While your engineers review code, an agent drafts copy and another plans the rollout. Relay coordinates them so nothing collides.

🎯

Agents you can direct

Tag an agent by name, hand it a task, and watch it report back into the room. You stay in control — the agents do the heavy lifting.

🔒

Roles & permissions

Control who can summon agents, approve work, and edit shared artifacts. Agents respect the same permissions your team does.

📜

Every decision recorded

The room is the source of truth. Scroll back through any decision, handoff, or agent action — nothing happens in a black box.

How it works

From invite to shipped, in minutes.

Step 1

Create your room

Spin up a workspace for a project, a sprint, or a launch. Invite your teammates by link — no installs, no setup.

Step 2

Add your agents

Bring in the agents you need — a planner, a copywriter, a code reviewer. Give each a name, a role, and the tools it can use.

Step 3

Work together, live

Tag an agent, assign a task, and watch it work alongside your team in real time. Review, redirect, and ship — all in one room.
